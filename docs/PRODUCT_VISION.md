# Product Vision

Professional, cross-platform desktop application for AI-assisted creative production.

Unified creative environment: generate, edit, organize, transform, reuse digital assets. Conversational + specialized workspaces. Asset management with lineage. Extensible platform with provider-agnostic execution (local/studio/cloud/ComfyUI).

Core philosophy: make complex creative production feel simple without removing professional capabilities. Desktop is product experience; AI/GPU/infra are independent and interchangeable.

Target users: creators, artists, 3D artists, video producers, agencies, studios, technical artists.
